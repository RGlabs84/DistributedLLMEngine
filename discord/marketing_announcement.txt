🚀 **Introducing DLNE (Distributed LLM Network Engine)**
Hey everyone! I've been quietly working on something massive in the background, and we are finally ready to open the doors. 

DLNE is a zero-configuration, decentralized engine that democratizes AI compute. It allows anyone—whether you have an RTX 4090 rig or just an old potato laptop—to instantly pool hardware resources and run massive open-source AI models as a single, unified supercomputer!

🌟 **What makes it special?**
- **Zero-Configuration Setup**: No complex port forwarding or DNS configs. Double-click the app, and you are instantly part of the swarm!
- **Cross-Platform & Hardware Agnostic**: We just finished building a deeply optimized compilation pipeline. DLNE natively supports **CUDA** (with split tiers for both Modern and Legacy NVIDIA cards), **Vulkan** (AMD/Intel), and **Pure CPU**. That old crypto mining rig collecting dust in your garage? It can become a huge asset here!
- **The "Client Portal"**: You never have to memorize IPs. DLNE silently spins up a background proxy on localhost. If the network fails over to a new Coordinator, your browser seamlessly follows it. Your chat history is never lost!
- **Democratized Model Voting**: Want to swap from a 7B model to a 70B model? The network dynamically calculates the total pooled VRAM across all active nodes and unlocks models that fit. When someone requests a swap, a frosted-glass modal pops up on everyone's screen to cast a vote!
- **Ollama Drop-In Replacement**: Have existing frontends like AnythingLLM or SillyTavern? DLNE acts as a native drop-in replacement for Ollama! Just point your apps to `localhost:11434` and they will instantly connect to the entire distributed swarm.

💻 **How it Works**
DLNE sits on top of a custom backend and uses blazing-fast gRPC to shard the model layers across your physical devices. We are actively developing a sleek, one-shot Windows Installer to make joining the swarm completely frictionless—complete with sliders so you can visually configure exactly how many cores or how much VRAM you want to donate.
