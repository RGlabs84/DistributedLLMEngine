# ⚖️ DLNE Swarm Rules ⚖️
1. **Be Respectful:** We are a community building a decentralized future. Be kind to other nodes.
2. **No Malicious Traffic:** The network relies on trust. Any attempt to sniff, inject, or tamper with the WireGuard tunnel will result in a permanent ban from the swarm.
3. **Hardware Transparency:** Do not spoof your hardware metrics. If you allocate 8GB of VRAM to the pool, ensure it is actually available.
4. **Keep it Clean:** Use `#off-topic` for casual chatter. Keep `#architecture` focused on engineering.
5. **Report Bugs:** Post any errors, tracebacks, or connection issues in `#bug-reports`.
