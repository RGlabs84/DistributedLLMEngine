# 🚀 How to Join the Swarm
Welcome to the frontier! To get your node online and start contributing/using the network:

1. **Install the CLI:** We will be releasing the packages shortly.
2. **Configure your Role:** For the Alpha rollout, we are **only looking for Workers**. You will configure your node as a standard Worker to connect to the primary alpha cluster. 
3. **Generate Keys:** The network operates on encrypted VPN tunnels. You will generate your own local private WireGuard keys that never leave your machine to securely join the swarm.
4. **Boot the Node:** Run `dlne start` in your terminal. The CLI will automatically perform a hardware benchmark, connect to the VPN tunnel, and place you in the active swarm.

*Stay tuned in `#announcements` for the official Alpha release date!*
